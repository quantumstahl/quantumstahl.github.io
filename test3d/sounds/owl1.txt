Ah… so you are the little cat I’ve heard so much about.

I am Alder, keeper of this gate. I have watched over this path for many, many years. Through storms, summers… and one particularly unpleasant squirrel infestation.

Beyond this gate, the forest grows darker, and the creatures considerably less polite.

If you wish to pass, you must first prove that you are ready. Help the folk of Fox Meadow. Complete their tasks, and return to me when you have earned their trust.

Do that, and I shall open the gate.

And please… don’t climb it. The last adventurer tried. I still find pieces of him in the bushes.
